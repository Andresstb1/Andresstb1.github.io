Modal Simulation and Analysis of Integrated Photonic Couplers (DC & MMI)

View repo →

Designed and simulated directional couplers (DC) and multimode interference (MMI) devices on a silicon nitride (Si₃N₄/SiO₂) platform at 1550 nm using Tidy3D and GDSFactory. Successfully solved and analyzed complex electromagnetic mode distributions, distinguishing even/odd and higher-order quasi-TE and quasi-TM modes, and computed optical coupling parameters, including effective indices (n_eff), polarization fractions, and beating lengths (L_π) for full optical power transfer and MMI design.

Electromagnetic Mode Solving and Compact Modeling of Silicon Nitride Waveguides

View repo →

Conducted numerical electromagnetic mode solving and dispersion characterization of strip (deep-etched) and rib (shallow-etched) silicon nitride (Si₃N₄/SiO₂) waveguides at 1550 nm using Tidy3D and GDSFactory. Optimized finite-difference grid parameters through numerical convergence studies and evaluated geometric width dependencies alongside polarization fraction mappings (quasi-TE/TM). Formulated second-order polynomial compact models to extract modal effective indices (n_eff), optical group indices (n_g), and chromatic dispersion (D), while analyzing bent waveguide mode profiles to quantify curvature-induced mode-mismatch and radiation losses.

Design, EME Modeling, and Characterization of Si₃N₄ Directional and MMI Couplers

View repo →

Designed and modeled silicon nitride (Si₃N₄/SiO₂) directional couplers (DC) and multimode interference (MMI) devices operating in the 1500–1600 nm telecommunication band using Tidy3D and Eigenmode Expansion (EME) methods via GDSFactory. Computed the beating length (L_π) and coupling coefficient (K) through even/odd supermode decomposition for varying waveguide gaps (0.6–1.6 µm) and determined criteria for total optical cross-transfer (L = L_π), 3 dB power splitting (L = L_π/2), and uncoupled parallel routing thresholds (K ≤ 0.01 over chip-scale lengths). In parallel, modeled 2×2 and 1×4 MMI splitters based on self-imaging theory, analyzing modal interference profiles, optical excess loss, and transmission balance across output ports.

Modeling, Analytical Formulation, and Circuit-Level Simulation of Integrated Silicon Photonic MZIs

View repo →

Designed and modeled integrated Mach-Zehnder Interferometers (MZIs) based on silicon-on-insulator (SOI) and silicon nitride (Si₃N₄) waveguide cross-sections for telecommunication wavelengths near 1550 nm. Implemented analytical transfer matrix formulations alongside scattering-matrix circuit solvers (SAX and GDSFactory) to evaluate spectral responses across balanced, phase-shifted, delay-imbalanced (ΔL), and amplitude-unbalanced configurations. Quantified the impact of coupler splitting ratios (κ_a, κ_b), excess insertion losses, and propagation attenuation on extinction ratio, insertion loss, and free spectral range (FSR), analyzing dual-arm path length differences to match target phase conditions and sensing performance criteria.
